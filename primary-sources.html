How to use this register

Start with a record that shows a decision, a negotiating instruction, or a change in policy. Pair it with a counterpart record or outcome, and use speeches as context unless they make or explain a decision. A scanned folder is still a research lead unless a specific document and page have been identified. A primary-source entry may link an official cataloged publication rather than an image; see individual access notes.

For Rio, read the CEA/Economic Policy Council track alongside preparations, attendance, the push for a climate agreement, the biodiversity position and the outcome. The April 8 Boskin–Yeutter text is an internal memo, not a verified transcript of a public Boskin statement. The proposed space chapter centers commercial satellite launches, remote sensing, international communications and export licensing; adjacent strategic, scientific, and bilateral material is context.

FRUS precedents and search limits

Nixon–Ford E–1, document 298 and document 303 show how the series documented preconference institutions and negotiating choices; E–3’s preface treats multilateral negotiations alongside the participants who changed them. Reagan XLI’s preface sets an explicit ozone/bilateral-scope boundary; document 364 models a decision-ready record on interagency differences and negotiating posture. The relevant Carter volume is not publicly available as a selection model.

Searches used official Public Papers, Bush Library/NARA record and scan pages, State FOIA, and official multilateral and federal publication sources. The State FOIA UNCED-specific log search found a case number, not a responsive document inventory. The Cheney public-index and small sample check does not establish a comprehensive search. Use NARA Scout’s Bush-era Rio query for follow-up; the query returned 272 unique catalog matches across 70 collections. These results include restricted records and withdrawals, not 272 documents selected for this register. A focused review promoted specific catalog locators to research leads. The live Scout tool is not part of the offline copy.
